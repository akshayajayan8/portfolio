WHO AM I?

I build the invisible. (the magical software kind)

I’m Akshay Ajayan. I build the invisible magical things called software — which, sadly, we still can’t tell whether they are soft, hard, or brittle by touching them.

Oh yes, my ability to build these things was approved by the big approving authority: Kerala Technological University, which gave me a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering with a rating of 7.97 / 10. So close to 8. Apparently, “hard work” has decimal places.

And no, I don’t only build software. I also draw, capture moments, edit videos, and mentor people. I like building magical things not only for myself, but for anyone who has an idea and wants to bring it to life. (Absolutely — magic requires currency spells.)

You might not be able to feel what I build, but I can assure you: it won’t be brittle, and it doesn’t have to be hard as a rock. I like my software soft enough to evolve, solid enough to work, and flexible enough to fine-tune.

The description of me could go on forever. But I’d rather build enough things to make “AKSHAY AJAYAN” a name you remember.
